How it's calculated

There is no single official standard: pattern companies and batting makers publish slightly different numbers. The sizes above are common starting points. Mattresses today are often 12" to 16" deep, so older charts with a 10" drop can look short on a modern bed.

quilt width = mattress width + 2 × drop

quilt length = mattress length + drop (foot) + pillow tuck

The drop is how far the quilt hangs over the side of the mattress. Measure from the top edge of the mattress to where you want the quilt to stop: about the mattress depth for a coverlet that just covers the mattress, more to cover a box spring, and all the way to the floor for a bedspread. The pillow tuck is extra length (about 10") that folds under and over the pillows; skip it if you use the quilt folded at the foot of the bed.

Worked example: a queen mattress is 60" × 80". With a 12" drop and a 10" pillow tuck the quilt should finish at 60 + 24 = 84" wide and 80 + 12 + 10 = 102" long. Quilting shrinks a quilt by roughly 3 to 5% (more with dense quilting and cotton batting that is washed), so add a few inches if you want to be sure of the size.

Once you know the size, work out the backing, binding and borders.

Frequently asked questions

What size is a queen quilt?

A queen quilt is usually about 90 x 95 to 90 x 108 inches for a 60 x 80 inch queen mattress, depending on how much drop and pillow tuck you want.

What size is a throw quilt?

Throws are usually 50 x 65 to 60 x 70 inches, big enough to cover an adult on the sofa.

What is the drop on a quilt?

The drop is the part that hangs over the side of the mattress. A coverlet drop is about the mattress depth, often 12 to 16 inches; a bedspread drops to the floor.

What is a pillow tuck?

About 10 extra inches of length at the head of the quilt that tucks under the pillows and folds over them.
