How it's calculated

All these methods start with squares and use a 1/4" seam. The added amounts come from geometry: a half square triangle loses 1/4" of seam on its diagonal plus the length of the point that sticks out past the seam, which works out to 7/8" more than the finished size. A quarter square triangle is cut twice on the diagonal, so it needs 1 1/4".

HST 2-at-a-time: square = finished + 7/8"

HST 4-at-a-time: square = (finished + 7/8") × 1.414, rounded up to 1/8"

HST 8-at-a-time: square = (finished + 7/8") × 2

QST (hourglass): square = finished + 1 1/4"

Flying geese, no waste: large = finished width + 1 1/4"; small = finished height + 7/8"

2-at-a-time: layer two squares, draw a diagonal, sew 1/4" on both sides, cut on the line. 4-at-a-time: sew all the way around the edge of two layered squares and cut on both diagonals; the exact size is (finished + 7/8") × √2, and the edges of the units are on the bias, so handle gently. 8-at-a-time: sew 1/4" each side of both diagonals, then cut horizontally, vertically and on both diagonals.

Worked example: for 3" finished HSTs, cut 3 7/8" squares for 2-at-a-time, 5 1/2" squares (5.48" exact) for 4-at-a-time, or 7 3/4" squares for 8-at-a-time. Every unit should then measure 3 1/2" before it is sewn into the block.

The flying geese method makes four geese from one large square and four small squares. For 4" × 2" geese, cut a 5 1/4" large square and four 2 7/8" small squares. Many quilters add 1/4" to any of these sizes and trim the units down afterward; tick the box to do that.

Frequently asked questions

Why do you add 7/8 inch for half square triangles?

The diagonal seam takes up fabric, and the triangle points extend past the seam line. Together that adds exactly 7/8 inch to the finished size when you cut squares and sew two HSTs at a time.

Should I cut HSTs oversized and trim?

Many quilters do. Adding 1/4 inch and trimming each unit to the unfinished size gives very accurate HSTs, at the cost of a little fabric and time.

What size do I cut for 8 at a time HSTs?

Double the finished size plus 7/8 inch: (finished + 7/8) x 2. For 2 inch finished HSTs that is a 5 3/4 inch square, which makes eight units.

What size square for a quarter square triangle?

Finished size plus 1 1/4 inches. For a 4 inch finished hourglass, cut 5 1/4 inch squares. One square of each fabric makes two hourglass units.

How do I make no-waste flying geese?

Cut one large square at finished width plus 1 1/4 inch and four small squares at finished height plus 7/8 inch. This makes four geese with no leftover triangles.
